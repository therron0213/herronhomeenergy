Home inspection services

A detailed look at the home before you make important decisions.

A residential home inspection reviews the major visible and accessible systems and components of the property, with findings explained in straightforward language.

Structure & exterior

Visible structural components, siding, grading, drainage, windows, doors, and other accessible exterior conditions.

Roof & attic

Accessible roofing, flashing, attic conditions, insulation, and ventilation.

Electrical & plumbing

Visible electrical and plumbing components, operation, and common safety or condition concerns.

Heating & cooling

Accessible HVAC equipment and a practical look at how major systems are operating.
