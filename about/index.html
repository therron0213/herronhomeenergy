About Herron Home Energy

Home inspection expertise backed by building-performance experience.

Herron Home Energy provides professional residential home inspections and manufactured home foundation certifications. A background in energy auditing and building performance adds a useful whole-home perspective to the inspection process.

Meet Tyler Herron

Herron Home Energy is led by Tyler Herron, an InterNACHI® Certified Professional Inspector (CPI®) and a Building Performance Institute (BPI) Certified Energy Auditor.

Tyler’s building-performance background adds a systems-based perspective to home inspections, with added context around insulation, ventilation, HVAC, moisture, comfort, and overall home performance.

Manufactured home foundation certifications are also available to document that a foundation meets applicable HUD standards.

The goal is simple: provide clear information, useful context, and professional service without unnecessary jargon.
