Residential Home Inspection

What the inspection covers

A whole-home inspection of the major visible and accessible systems and components of the property. Findings are communicated in straightforward language so you can better understand the condition of the home.

Common areas reviewed

Structure

Visible structural components and signs of damage or movement.

Exterior

Siding, trim, grading, drainage, doors, windows, and other accessible exterior components.

Roof & attic

Accessible roofing, flashing, attic conditions, insulation, and ventilation.

Electrical

Visible service equipment, panels, receptacles, and common safety concerns.

Plumbing

Visible supply, drain, fixtures, water heating, and signs of leakage.

Heating & cooling

Accessible HVAC equipment and general operating condition.

The exact scope of an inspection depends on the property, accessibility, and the inspection agreement.
